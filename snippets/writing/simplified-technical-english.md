Write documentation in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English. One approved meaning per word,
one topic per sentence, active voice, present tense, and an explicit subject on every
instruction. Keep instruction sentences short and descriptive sentences only slightly longer.

Say the same thing the same way every time. A synonym reads as a new concept to a reader who is
scanning or translating, and to an agent matching on wording. Reserve technical terms for the
things they actually name, and drop the rest of the vocabulary to plain approved words.
