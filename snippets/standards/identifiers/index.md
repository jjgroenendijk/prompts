# Identifiers

* [BCP 47 Language Tags](bcp-47-language-tags.md) - Identify languages with BCP 47 tags, lowercase language and uppercase region.
* [ISO 3166-1 Country Codes](iso-3166-country-codes.md) - Abbreviate countries with ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes, uppercase, two letters.
* [RFC 4122 GUID Format](rfc-4122-guids.md) - Write GUIDs in RFC 4122 canonical form, lowercase hex, hyphenated, no braces.
* [UN/LOCODE Location Codes](un-locode-locations.md) - Abbreviate places with UN/LOCODE rather than an invented site or office code.
