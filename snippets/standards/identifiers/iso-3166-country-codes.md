Abbreviate every country with its ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code. Two letters, uppercase, exactly as
the standard lists it.

Do not mix in alpha-3 codes, numeric codes, top-level domain suffixes, or an abbreviation of the
country name. One list, one form, so codes compare and sort predictably wherever they appear.
